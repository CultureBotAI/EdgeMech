# Common occurrence-review rules

Assess the exact original proposition, predicate, endpoint identities and
definitions. Keep claim support, experimental literature, relation modeling,
each endpoint and source fidelity as six separate judgments. Evidence must be
appropriate to the original source, producer and ontology version. A later
correction does not alter the original assertion.

Source fidelity asks whether the occurrence preserves the meaning of its
particular source assertion. Exact bytes prove preservation; semantic support
requires the source meaning and transformation; independent biological truth
is a separate question. Identify the exact source version, record, selected
field, identity chain and relevant transformation. Preserve aggregation,
polarity, specificity, units and qualifiers.

- `SUPPORTED`: exact source and necessary identities/field meanings are bound,
  and the assertion preserves the source content at the stated scope.
- `CONTRADICTED`: bound semantic witnesses demonstrate an unfaithful
  transformation. State the specific meaning changed and how the selected
  source field and transformation are established. This judgment does not
  require or establish biological falsity.
- `NOT_ASSESSABLE`: a necessary source, identity, field-selection, definition,
  unit, qualifier or transformation witness is missing or internally
  conflicting. Identify the precise missing witness. An unproved derivation
  cannot establish a particular transformation error.
- `UNASSESSED`: work not performed; disallowed in completed initial matrices.

An established mapping that adds unjustified specificity can be unfaithful
even if the narrower biological proposition could independently be true.
Generic wording alone does not establish a narrower phenotype, and missing
specificity does not establish its biological opposite. Formal graph modeling
problems affect source fidelity only when they change represented source
meaning; do not copy a modeling category automatically.

Different statistics and measurements remain distinct. A minimum, maximum,
growth observation, optimum, range, bin membership, fractional weight and
prevalence do not substitute for one another. Preserve supplied units and
percentage bases. Do not invent conversions or assay conditions. Determine
whether missing context is necessary for the exact assertion.

Bind the identity chain actually used by the occurrence. Do not select whichever
conflicting identity gives a favorable result. An unrelated crosswalk problem
does not automatically invalidate an independently established literal endpoint
chain. Explain participating fields and residual conflicts. A paper about an
organism cannot assign an ambiguous source datum to that organism. Producer
quarantine establishes a disposition, not the original property's truth.

`assertion_basis: source_report` is permitted only when the original proposition
is about what an identified source reports. It cannot recast a biological
property, replace a predicate/endpoint or fill missing conditions. Resolved
reports require the original source instance plus independent, versioned
original identity and definition witnesses, explicitly grouped and bound to the
claim. Authorities establish meaning; source-instance evidence establishes the
reported content. Every linked authority, including contrary authority, enters
the applicable witness groups. Missing original identity or meaning stays
unresolved. Paper counterevidence retains its biological interpretation.

Literature evidence must be replayable paper text, with primary publication
identity, actual organism/strain, inspected extent and appropriate conditions.
An abstract remains an abstract. Source rows, dataset provenance and producer
documentation are not paper support. Report-only support never counts toward
the independently paper-backed directional-claim gate.

Bind every necessary witness before sealing: original source, identities,
definitions and producer rules where needed. A rationale mentioning evidence
is not an explicit proof binding. Every proof applicable to an axis must be
in that axis's canonical evidence set and mapped to its manifest evidence ID
in the matrix. Complete drafts, sidecars and matrix derive from the same
authored judgments. Later augmentation belongs to distinct adjudication.

Missing evidence is an assessed limit, not refutation. Supported content may
coexist with incorrect or unresolved modeling only when the exact content
proposition is established. Explain its scope; do not change it to make a save
succeed. Successful validation/persistence proves neither entailment nor
scientific approval. Agreement on unknowns does not satisfy resolution floors.
Never select labels to meet thresholds.
