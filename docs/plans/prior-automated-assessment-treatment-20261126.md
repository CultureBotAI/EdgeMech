Scientific adequacy and prior-assessment membership are different questions.
The authorized baseline includes prior partial and unresolved assessments; it
does not require a prior assessment to be correct. The proposed treatment is
therefore **occurrence-scoped automated prior-assessment candidates**, retaining
all records and their actual limitations. Automation, failed scientific quality
or a generic rationale alone must not silently turn a prior categorical judgment
into an unreviewed occurrence. At the same time, source/cohort labels and copied
annotations do not prove an independently authored judgment on every target.
Independent baseline review must resolve that distinction explicitly.
