Three distinct questions

What was in the release?

Each row identifies an original edge occurrence. “Retained” and omission reasons describe the curated derivative. A recorded rule label is provenance, not an independently validated scientific verdict.

What did the source report?

Source fidelity asks whether the original claim preserves its source's entities, direction, and meaning. The 100 retained canary records are historical assessments by automated reviewers.

What supports the claim?

Scientific support is a separate recorded assessment. “Not found” means unresolved within a bounded search; “not assessable” means the available evidence could not decide. Neither means the claim is false.

These two purposive 50-occurrence cohorts do not estimate population accuracy. The other 9,900 sample occurrences have no assessment in these retained cohorts. This page does not incorporate open review branches or certify independent calibration.

Native-review transfer is another, separate contract: identical native assertion and scope bindings are required. Endpoint or triple matches, transport validity, and existing rule labels cannot establish scientific approval.
